Simplified Workflow

How ExpiryAlert Works

Five quick steps that bridge retail inventory safety with consumer notification pipelines.

Step 1

Register Product

Staff registers new stock batches with their expiry dates.

Step 2

Generate QR

System yields unique batch QR code identifiers.

Step 3

Scan on Billing

QR code is scanned at billing counters to add items.

Step 4

Receive Invoice

Customer receives custom generated bills on email/portal.

Step 5

Expiry Alerts

System sends automated SMS/emails before expiration.
